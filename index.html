Selection rules & conference champions

2026–27 format: champions of the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12 and ACC get auto bids, plus the highest-ranked Group of 6 team, plus Notre Dame if it's in the top 12. The rest of the 12 spots go to the next-highest-ranked teams. Teams are seeded by ranking, except the top Group of 6 team, which always gets the 12 seed. Seeds 1–4 get byes. No reseeding.
